# Recreation Prompt: "Relationship Consultant" Hindi Reel (63 s, 9:16)

> How to use this: Section A is the master prompt. Paste it into an AI video or editing agent (CapCut AI, Descript Underlord, HyperFrames, Runway, etc.) or give it to a human editor. Section B has a separate image/video generation prompt for each AI B-roll shot. Section C is the exact motion-graphics spec, timed to the second.
> You must supply your own talking-head footage (the host). The cinematic B-roll shots in the original are AI-generated images with a slow camera move, so you can regenerate them from the prompts in Section B.

---

## A. MASTER PROMPT

Create a 63-second vertical (1080×1920, 9:16, 30 fps) Hindi/Hinglish Instagram Reel for a relationship and astrology consultant. Its style is cinematic, emotional, premium and "dark-academia studio". The edit alternates between:
1. **Host A-roll**: a bearded man in clear rectangular glasses and a dark navy pinstripe shirt, sitting in a cream armchair. The studio has dark navy-blue wainscot panelling, dark wooden bookshelves holding antique brass objects (sextant, armillary globe, telescope) and moody low-key lighting with a dark vignette at the bottom. There are two camera angles: **front medium-wide** and **side-profile close-up** (the 3/4 profile, looking left).
2. **AI-generated cinematic B-roll** of a sad young Indian woman in a teal-green kurta, in warm golden-hour Indian home interiors. The colours are warm amber against teal clothing, with soft film grain.

**Global look**
- Colour grade: host shots are cool and desaturated, with navy shadows crushed slightly. B-roll is warm orange/amber with a teal accent, using the classic teal-and-orange look.
- Add a subtle dark gradient over the bottom 35% of host shots so the white captions stay readable.
- Light film grain over everything. Every shot has a slow, constant scale (Ken Burns push-in, 100%→108%). No shot is ever fully static.
- Transitions are never hard cuts between "worlds" (host ↔ B-roll). Always use a **white flash / bokeh light-leak burst** lasting 6–10 frames: the frame blows out to white with soft teal-white circular bokeh orbs, then resolves into the next shot. Cuts between host angles are hard cuts.

**Caption / kinetic-typography system (the most important part)**
- Every spoken phrase gets an animated caption at **lower-centre**, about 62–72% of frame height, sitting on the host's chest.
- Each caption has a **hero word** and **small support words**:
  - The hero word is LARGE (about 110–130 px), white, in a **thin/light geometric sans with a single-storey "a"**. Close matches are Questrial, Glacial Indifference, Century Gothic or Lexend Light. It has a very soft dark drop-shadow.
  - Support words are SMALL (about 28–34 px), in the same font, white. They sit tucked **above-right and below-left** of the hero word, overlapping its edges. Example: small "partner" sits on top of a big "Aapka".
- Animation: words appear **one by one, synced to speech** (word-level timing). Each word fades in from 0% to 100% opacity with a **blur-in** (Gaussian 20 px→0) and a slight scale-down (110%→100%) over 6–8 frames. The hero word lands with a soft "pop" ease (easeOutBack). When the phrase ends, the whole caption blurs and fades out over 5 frames while the next one builds.
- Accent styles used in specific places:
  - **Teal highlighter pill**: a white sans-serif label inside a rounded rectangle of teal-green (#0F9A78 to #14A982). It is revealed by a **left-to-right mask wipe** with a bright white light-sweep along the leading edge.
  - **Text behind subject (depth effect)**: big titles placed BEHIND the host's head and shoulders using a subject mask/rotoscope, so his head covers part of the letters.
  - **Script accent words** in an elegant italic serif script such as Playfair Display Italic or Great Vibes ("Pishe", "couples").
  - **Ultra-condensed thin all-caps display** in a font such as Bebas Neue Thin, Antonio Thin or Big Shoulders Display Thin ("10 SAAL").
  - **Gradient-filled hero word**: navy-to-sky-blue vertical gradient, semi-transparent ("Kya").

**Recurring overlays**
- **Neon question mark**: an outlined "?" with an electric-blue neon tube glow (#3FB8FF core, #0A6CFF outer glow). It **flickers on** like a neon sign (on/off/on over 8 frames), then bobs gently up and down. It floats above the head of the person on screen.
- **B&W cut-out people**: desaturated (black-and-white) cut-out photos of an arguing couple, a woman on the left with hand on hip and a man on the right pointing. They slide in from the frame edges behind the host, placed between the host and the background.
- **Memory clouds**: torn-paper or cloud-shaped photo bubbles in teal-grey that drift upward from the woman's head and grow in size. The last and largest one contains a happy couple selfie (a "memory").
- **Halo glow**: a pale cyan-white circular glow disc with a soft outer ring that fades in behind the sad woman's head.
- **Memory projection**: a semi-transparent warm-toned image of the couple projected on the wall, with burnt-film or fire-edge light leaks around it.

**Audio**: male voiceover in conversational Hindi, with a soft, emotional, slow piano/ambient pad underneath at about -22 dB. Add a "whoosh" + "shimmer" SFX on every white-flash transition, a soft "pop" or "tick" on each hero word, and a neon "buzz" when the question mark flickers on.

## E. EDITOR QUICK-SETTINGS (CapCut / Premiere / AE)

- **Caption font**: Questrial (or Glacial Indifference), with hero word 120 px and support words 30 px, tracking -10.
- **Caption in**: Blur 20→0, Opacity 0→100, Scale 110→100, 7 frames, easeOutBack. **Caption out**: Blur 0→15, Opacity 100→0, 5 frames.
- **Teal pill**: #0F9A78 fill, 12 px corner radius, 8 × 18 px padding, linear wipe L→R over 10 frames plus a white 30° gradient sweep.
- **Neon "?"**: 3 px white stroke, outer glow #1E90FF at 40 px and 80 px, flicker keyframes on, off, on, off, on over the first 8 frames, then a ±8 px Y-bob on a 1.5 s loop.
- **Transitions**: "Light Leak / Bokeh Flash" overlay in Screen blend mode, 8 frames, peaking at 100% white on the cut frame.
- **Text-behind-subject**: duplicate the host layer, apply AI subject cutout / Roto Brush, and place it above the text layer.
- **Shots**: every shot gets a 100→108% scale over its duration. Host shots get a bottom black gradient at 60%.
