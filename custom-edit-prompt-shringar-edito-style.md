## 4. Framing, camera moves and depth (rotoscoping)

### 4a. Punch-ins: the reference "AFTER" footage is always punched in
- **Scene S default framing:** a **1.30× medium close-up**, waist up, centred on her chest. Her eyes sit at about **30–33% of frame height**, which leaves headroom above her for the 3D word.
- **Tighter jump-cut zoom:** **1.45×**, re-centred on her face. Never go beyond 1.5×. Use a detail-preserving AI upscale on punch-ins over 1.35×.
- **Slow push-in on every held segment:** +4% scale over the segment (e.g. 1.30 → 1.35), easeInOut.
- **Jump-cut zoom:** a hard cut from the current scale to +12–15% tighter, **on a pause or word boundary only**. On that same frame, **all previous text clears instantly** (no exit animation). This is a signature move of the reference.
- **Scene N:** after stabilising, use 1.10× as the base and 1.22× for the jump-cut zoom.

## 7. Sound design

- **Voice:** her original lav audio **is** the voiceover. Don't replace it or generate a new one.
  - Apply a high-pass at 80 Hz and **gentle** street-noise reduction on Scene N (not underwater-sounding).
  - De-ess, then compress at 3:1.
  - Match Scene N and Scene S in loudness and tone, with a 4-frame room-tone crossfade across the whip.
  - The source is hot (−11.5 LUFS), so bring it down and don't clip.
- **Music:** an **upbeat, modern Indian-fusion pop beat** (a bansuri/flute hook for the Krishna theme, tabla/dholak plus a punchy kick and claps), about 110–120 BPM, royalty-free.
  - Keep it 18–22 dB under the voice, with sidechain ducking (−5 dB while she speaks).
  - Edit it so the **3D slams (0.6 s, 19.35 s)**, the **whip (11.3 s)**, the **logo-scene flash (≈26.0 s)** and the **"JODHPUR" burst (≈29.9 s)** land on **downbeats** (time-stretch ±3% if needed).
  - End on a clean music button at about 31.2 s.
- **SFX on every pop** (−22 to −16 dB, never covering words; vary the pitch so it doesn't get repetitive):
  - **whoosh** on every text reveal and exit
  - **pop/click** on every tile and icon
  - **glass clink** on the thumbnails
  - **riser + impact** on both 3D slams and the final "JODHPUR"
  - **temple-bell "ting"** on the diya tile and the badge
  - **typing ticks** on the typed line
  - **brush swish** on the swoosh
  - **petal-burst whoosh + shimmer** at ≈29.9 s
- **Master:** **−14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP**.

## 8. Things to avoid
- Anything teal, mint or dark green (that's the reference palette).
- Any price, "₹", coin or banknote, rating, follower count, "Best"/"No.1" claim or invented offer.
- Running word-by-word subtitles at the bottom. The reference is **graphic-led**: every phrase gets a designed treatment (gradient words, labels, write-ons), not a subtitle bar.
- Text over her face, or any graphic crossing her mouth while she speaks.
- Matte chatter or halos around her hair and fingers on the behind-hands and behind-hair beats.
- Text left on screen across a jump-cut zoom (it must clear on the cut frame).
- Shaky night footage: stabilise Scene N before adding any tracked graphics.
