## 4. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM (THE MOST IMPORTANT PART; replicate exactly)

### A. TOP HEADLINE: the main keyword
- **Position:** upper third, centred, **y ≈ 20–30%** of frame height. Her head should sit at or just below the bottom of the word.
- **Font:** ultra-heavy slanted geometric sans: **Poppins Black Italic** (fallbacks: Montserrat Black Italic, Gilroy Heavy Italic). Tracking **−20 to −40**.
- **Size:** huge, **80–90% of frame width** (≈ 860–970 px wide). Short words (e.g. "JDA", "₹") scale up to fill ~70% of width but never exceed ~420 px cap height.
- **Fill:** gold→saffron gradient (§3) with the plum soft-shadow halo.
- **Depth effect (signature look):**
  - The headline goes **BEHIND the presenter**. Her head, hair and sunglasses overlap the letters.
  - Generate a per-frame person matte with MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation, rembg/U²-Net-human, RobustVideoMatting or SAM2. This is easy here: a clean blue sky sits behind her head in nearly every shot.
  - Composite order: background plate → headline → presenter matte → lower captions.
  - Feather the matte 2–3 px and temporally smooth it (EMA or 3-frame median) to avoid edge chatter.
  - In the wide shots (S2, S5 far), she is too small to overlap. Place the headline lower so it still sits **behind her upper body/head** where possible. Otherwise use it plain, in front.
- **Sub-word:** a small secondary word in **Poppins Light Italic / ExtraLight Italic, white**, about **30% of headline cap height**.
  - It tucks into the **lower-right corner of the headline**, slightly overlapping it.
  - It carries a 15% black soft shadow for legibility.
  - Examples: *Jodhpur ka*, *Banar mein*, *sirf*, *se seedha*, *wide*, *approved*, *loanable*, *wala*, *plot*, *ka mauka*.
- **Optional pre-word:** small white light italic placed **above-left** of the headline (e.g. *"Jodhpur ka"* above **"BANAR"**, or *"sirf"* above **"3 KM"**).

### B. LOWER CAPTION: chest level, IN FRONT of the presenter
- **Position:** **y ≈ 55–65%**, centred over her chest/brochure.
- **Main word:** **Poppins ExtraBold / Montserrat Black**, upright or slightly italic, ~45–55% of headline size.
- **Fill:** **duotone split**. The top half is **plum #3A1236**; the bottom half is a **royal blue #1E5FD1 → sky blue #2FA4F0** gradient.
  - The split line is a soft diagonal or wave mask that **animates across the word** (see §5).
  - Add a subtle moving inner shine: a 20% white diagonal sheen sweep.
  - Add a thin 2 px white outer stroke at 60% so the plum reads on dark trousers and the blue reads against the denim shirt.
- **Small white helper words above** (Poppins SemiBold, white, ~35% of main word size), e.g. *agla*, *apna*, *pura*, *seedha*.
- **Small white helper words below** (Poppins Light, white), e.g. *kar lijiye*, *scheme*, *roads*, *se judaav*, *uplabdh*, *wala plot*.
- **Karaoke fillers:** tiny white **Poppins Regular** single words (~60–70 px) flash one at a time, **in sync with speech**, centred at chest level.
  - Each lasts only **4–8 frames**.
  - Use them on connective words between keywords: *"aur"*, *"bhi"*, *"yahan"*, *"sirf"*, *"hai"*, *"se"*, *"ki"*, *"pe"*, *"abhi"*.
  - Aim for 6–10 such flashes in total. Do not overdo them.

1. **Local-pride variant:** the word **"JODHPUR"** (or **"RAJASTHAN"**) in the headline style. Swap the fill for a **Rajasthani sunset gradient** `#FFD34D → #F28C28 → #C2185B` (top→bottom), with the same plum halo. This replaces the reference's tricolour "India's".

## 5. TEXT ANIMATION RULES (timing at 30 fps)

- **Headline IN (8–10 frames):**
  - scale **130% → 95% → 100%** (overshoot pop, easeOutBack)
  - opacity 0 → 100
  - directional motion blur (horizontal, 20 px → 0)
  - slight 3D Y-rotation **−15° → 0°**
  - **The pop lands exactly on the spoken syllable AND on the nearest music kick** (§9). If they conflict, prioritise the syllable and nudge the music.
- **Sub-word IN:** starts **4–6 frames after** the headline. It slides **20 px from the right** with a fade, or does a fast type-on (1 char per frame).
- **Pre-word IN:** 2–3 frames before the headline, fades down 10 px.
- **Lower caption IN:** word-by-word.
  - Each word fades up with **Gaussian blur 20 → 0** and **scale 90% → 100%** over 6–8 frames.
  - At the same time the **duotone mask wipes left → right over 10–12 frames**, so the word shimmers plum → blue.
  - Helper words above and below come in 2–3 frames later with a simple fade + 8 px drift.
- **Karaoke filler:** hard-cut on (no fade), hold 4–8 frames, hard-cut off, with a 1-frame 105% → 100% scale tick.
- **Hold:** the headline **holds for the whole phrase (1.5–4 s)** with a slow **2–3% scale-up drift**. Lower captions **change underneath** while the headline stays.
- **OUT:** fast blur-out (blur 0 → 25) with an opacity drop over **5–6 frames**, OR the text is wiped away by the next transition (flash or glitch). Text must never linger over a hard cut into B-roll, unless it is deliberately carried across a flash.
