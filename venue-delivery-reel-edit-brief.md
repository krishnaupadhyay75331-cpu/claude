## 3. COLOUR GRADE (built around this footage's palette)

Palette to protect: **pearl white car · electric royal blue satin · pink saree with a red blouse · deep magenta/crimson roses · red carpet · warm golden fairy lights · night amber street lights.**

- **Overall:** a clean, bright "showroom commercial" look. White balance ~5600 K with +5 warmth. The showroom footage is lit by cool white LEDs, so warm it gently and keep the car **pure white with no blue cast**.
- **Contrast:** medium-high, with lifted mids. Deep blacks that are not crushed (especially the night shots, 17–18). Roll off the highlights softly: the white car and the ceiling lights must not clip.
- **Selective saturation:**
  - Magentas/pinks/reds (saree, roses, carpet): **+15 to +20**, hue nudged slightly toward magenta so the saree and roses match.
  - **The blue satin (shot 4 and the first frames of 8): keep it rich electric blue (+10)**. It is the "cool before the warm" contrast, the role the grey stormy sky plays in the reference.
  - Yellows/oranges (fairy lights, the yellow flowers on the garland, street lights): +10.
  - Greens (trees, shots 3 and 5): −15 and slightly toward olive.
  - **Skin:** natural and warm. Keep it on the skin-tone line and check it after the magenta push.
- **Mood shot (3):** cool, desaturated (~−50%), high contrast, with only the warm lights surviving.
- **Night shots (17–18):** keep the blacks clean. Make the white car and the DRLs pop with a slight glow/bloom on the lights (+10).
- **Sharpening:** +20–30 (the "iPhone HDR" crisp look). Add light denoise on the night shots first.
- **Signature bottom gradient on EVERY shot:** a linear black gradient from 0% at ~75% of the frame height to ~85% black at the bottom edge.
- **LUT reference:** a clean "commercial / Kodak 2383 lite" at 25–30%, or CapCut "Clear / HD" plus "Vivid" at ~35%. **Avoid a heavy teal-orange look**: it turns the pink saree salmon and the white car teal.
