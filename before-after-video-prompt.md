# Prompt: "Before / After" Edit Showcase Reel (Edito Hub style)

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 720×1280 (or 1080×1920), 29.97 fps, **11.7 seconds**, no hard cuts on the outer frame. The whole video is one static "stage" with two cards on it.

---

## 1. The stage (constant for the full 11.7 s)

- **Background:** Smooth vertical gradient. Pure white (#FFFFFF) at the top third, fading to a mid-grey (#7A7A7A to #8C8C8C) at the bottom. It reads like a seamless studio floor. No texture and no vignette.
- **Two portrait video cards, side by side, slightly overlapping in depth:**
  - **BEFORE card (left, smaller, further back):** about 40% of frame width. It bleeds off the left edge (x ≈ 0–290 px on 720 wide) and sits lower (y ≈ 380–900). Rounded corners (~20 px). Very slight 3D Y-rotation, so the card angles gently toward the centre. Soft drop shadow.
  - **AFTER card (right, larger, in front):** about 55% of frame width. It bleeds off the right edge (x ≈ 320–720), is taller and sits higher (y ≈ 280–1000). Same rounded corners and a slight tilt: the top edge rises a little to the right. Stronger soft shadow. Its bottom edge fades into the grey floor.
- **Labels** above each card, in a heavy, wide, black geometric display font (Archivo Black, Druk Wide or Monument Extended style), all caps, with slight perspective to match each card:
  - "BEFORE": small, centred above the left card (y ≈ 355).
  - "AFTER": about 1.4× larger, centred above the right card (y ≈ 250).
- The cards and labels stay fixed for the whole video. Only the card contents change.

## 2. BEFORE card content (0:00–11.7 s, one continuous take)

- Raw, unedited talking-head footage. A woman presenter in a black kurta with a gold/olive embroidered panel stands in a warm, softly lit room: a beige wall with window-shadow light patterns, a lamp on the left and dried pampas grass on the right.
- Wide medium shot (knees up). Flat, slightly warm colour, no graphics, no zoom, no cuts. She speaks to camera with natural hand gestures, such as palms together and an open-hand "explaining" gesture.
- This is the **same take** as the AFTER card, playing in perfect sync. That shows the edit was done on the same source footage.

## 3. AFTER card content: the edited version, beat by beat

## 4. Motion and style rules

- Every pop-in uses **ease-out-back** (about 10–15% overshoot) at 6–8 frames. Every exit uses **directional blur plus fade** at 4–6 frames.
- Apply **motion blur** to all moving graphics.
- Held graphics get a subtle idle float (±4 px, ±2° 3D rotation, about a 2 s loop).
- Text sits *behind* the presenter's hands or body where it overlaps. Use roto or a person mask for depth.
- Colour palette: indigo/violet (#3A2BD8, #7A5CFF), mint/teal (#2E8F86), dark green (#1F5A45), black, white and grey.
- The AFTER footage is graded brighter, cleaner and slightly cooler than the warm, flat BEFORE footage.

## 5. Audio

- A Hinglish female voiceover drives the edit. Its lines match the on-screen words: *"Sirf 299 rupees… video editing… creators aur brands ke liye… Edito Hub ke saath editing… starting just 299 rupees."*
- Upbeat, modern background music.
- **Sound effects** on every pop: whoosh on text reveals, pop/click on icons, riser plus impact on "299", and a cash/money-burst sound at 10.5 s.
