# Prompt: New Car Delivery Reel (Hyundai showroom, couple, 29 s)

## MASTER PROMPT (copy-paste)

> Make a 29-second vertical 9:16 (720×1280 or 1080×1920, 30 fps) cinematic **new-car delivery reel** of an Indian couple taking delivery of a white compact SUV (Hyundai Exter/Inster style, boxy front, horizontal LED DRL bar, H-pattern lights) at a Hyundai showroom.
> **Style:** premium Instagram "car delivery" reel. Clean, bright and slightly warm. Gentle slow motion on the people shots. Hard cuts on the beat. Short motion-blur whip transitions at energy changes. Two cut-out "pop-in" stickers: a steering wheel and a mini car. A dark gradient fades the bottom ~22% of every frame toward black. No text, no captions, no watermark, no logo.
> **Music:** a warm, melodic, romantic Indian (Hindi/Marathi) track at **~112 BPM**, mostly harmonic (strings/pads/guitar with a light beat). Soft intro for the first 10 s, a short build/riser at ~9–11 s, and a fuller "drop" landing exactly on the car unveil at **11.45 s**. It stays full until ~26 s, then fades out to silence by 29 s.
> **Cut rhythm:** after the drop, cut every 4 beats (~2.13 s). During the 17.8–20.0 s detail montage, cut on every beat (~0.53 s).
> **Story:** showroom sign → stormy sky with a bird → covered car → couple walks in → husband waits → wife signs papers → unveil with confetti → husband with keys/steering pop → quick detail montage → wife portrait → wife's pooja with mini-car pop → car driving out on the city road → highway → fade to black.

## COLOUR GRADE

- **Overall:** clean, bright "showroom commercial" look. Neutral-warm white balance (~5600 K, +5 to +10 warmth), crisp whites on the car and shirt.
- **Contrast:** medium-high, lifted mids, deep but not crushed blacks. Highlights rolled off softly (no clipped white).
- **Saturation:** selective. Boost **reds** (satin cover, roses, garland) and **yellows/oranges** (saree, DRLs, indicators) by +15–20. Keep greens and blues slightly muted. Keep skin tones natural and warm.
- **Sharpening:** strong (+20–30), the "phone HDR / iPhone cinematic" look.
- **Sky shots (3):** cool, desaturated, near-monochrome grey, high contrast for mood. This is a deliberate contrast with the colourful reveal.
- **Bottom gradient overlay on EVERY shot:** a linear black gradient from 0 % at ~75 % frame height to ~80–90 % black at the bottom edge. This is the signature of the edit.
- **LUT reference:** "Teal & Orange lite" at 30–40 % strength, or CapCut filter "Clear / HD" + "Vivid" at ~40 %.

## EFFECTS & TRANSITIONS

1. **Whip-pan / directional motion blur** (horizontal, 4–6 frames) at: 11.45 (drop/unveil), 17.80, 19.47, 20.00, 22.03.
2. **Cross-dissolve** sky → covered car at 2.93 (8–10 frames).
3. **Pop-in stickers** (cut-outs of real footage, background removed): a steering wheel at ~17.4 s and the decorated mini car at ~23.6 s. Animation: scale 0 % → 110 % → 100 % over ~6 frames, with a soft drop shadow.
4. **Slow motion** (50–60 %, optical-flow interpolated) on shots 5, 6, 7, 10, 15, 16.
5. **Confetti:** real confetti cannon at the unveil, or a confetti overlay (screen blend).
6. **Slow push-in / zoom** (100 → 108 %) on static shots for constant motion.
7. **Ending:** 0.7 s fade to black plus desaturation.
8. **No text, captions, logo or watermark anywhere.**
