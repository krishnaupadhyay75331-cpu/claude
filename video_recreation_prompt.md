# Video Recreation Prompt: "Ludhiana ka agla Landmark" Real-Estate Reel

## 1. Format & Overall Style
- **Format:** Vertical 9:16, 720×1280 (or 1080×1920), 30 fps, about 43.6 s long, stereo audio at 44.1 kHz.
- **Genre:** A fast, high-energy Instagram Reel / YouTube Short ad for a commercial real-estate project. A female presenter speaks to camera in Hinglish (Hindi + English, written in Roman script). Bold kinetic typography appears on nearly every spoken keyword.
- **Look:** Bright, airy and slightly overexposed daylight grade. Warm skin, lifted whites, low contrast and soft highlights. The background is shallow-focus (f/1.8–2.8 bokeh). Everything feels premium and lifestyle-led.
- **Presenter wardrobe/look (for consistency):** White strapless ruched top, tan/caramel high-waist paper-bag trousers with a tie belt, a chunky pearl necklace, large silver hoop earrings, long highlighted brown hair, and nude heels. A small lav mic is clipped to the top.
- **Camera:** Gimbal shots. The presenter is centred, either walking slowly toward the camera or standing with hands clasped at the waist. Medium-wide framing, with punch-in crops (110–130 %) on emphasis lines. Some wide full-body shots of her walking.

## 2. Typography System (the most important part)
Every caption follows a **two-tier system**:

### A. TOP HEADLINE (the main keyword, upper third, y ≈ 20–30 % of the frame)
- **Font:** Ultra-heavy, slanted geometric sans-serif, such as **Poppins Black Italic / Montserrat Black Italic / Gilroy Heavy Italic**. Tight tracking (−20 to −40).
- **Size:** Huge. The word fills about 80–90 % of the frame width.
- **Colour:** Peach-orange vertical gradient, **#F7B183 (top) → #E8793F (bottom)**.
- **Shadow:** A large, soft, dark drop shadow/glow behind the letters (black at 35–45 % opacity, blur 25–40 px, offset 6–10 px down-right). This makes a hazy dark halo that separates the text from the bright sky.
- **Depth effect:** The headline sits **BEHIND the presenter's head and hair** (rotoscope/subject mask). Her head overlaps the letters, as on "Ludhiana", "₹20", "15%", "India's" and "Smart".
- **Sub-word:** A small secondary word in **thin/light italic white sans (Poppins Light Italic / Montserrat Thin Italic)**, about 30 % of the headline's height. It tucks into the lower-right corner of the headline and overlaps it slightly. Examples: Ludhiana *ka*, yaha *apni*, Investment *starts*, loyalty *benefit*, Attractive *rental*, Jewellery *Hub*, Double-height *Shops*, Dawatpur *Punjab's*, 1000+ *Car*, Smart *investors*, ₹25,000 *monthly* / *income*, India's *next*.
- **Optional pre-word** (small white light italic, above-left): *Luxury* Heritage *Bazaar*, *sirf* ₹20.

### B. LOWER CAPTION (chest level, y ≈ 55–65 %, in FRONT of the presenter)
- **Main word:** Heavy upright/slightly italic sans (Poppins ExtraBold / Montserrat Black) with a **duotone split fill**. The top half is **deep plum/aubergine #3A0F33** and the bottom half is **cyan-blue #1B9AD6 → teal #2BB3C0**. The split is a soft diagonal or wave mask that animates across the word. There is a subtle inner shine.
  - Words: *Landmark, book, Matlab, fitout, Chowk, Bridal, destination, Parking, choose.*
- **Small white words above** (Poppins SemiBold, white, small): *agla*, *jagah*, *Till*, *after*, *Fountain*.
- **Small white words below** (Poppins Light, white): *kar lijiye*, *shopping*, *destination*, *mein se ek*, *spaces*, *karte hai*, *Lakh*.
- **Single-word karaoke fillers:** Tiny white Poppins Regular words flash one at a time in sync with the speech, centred at chest level. Examples: "Se", "Pehle", "De", "Har", "annum", "bigest", "nahi". Each lasts only 4–8 frames.

### C. SPECIAL VARIANTS
- **"India's"**: The headline fill is an **Indian tricolour gradient**, saffron #FF9933 → white → green #138808, from top to bottom.
- **"₹32 Lakh"**: "₹32" in the plum/blue duotone, large and lower-centre, with "Lakh" in thin white italic.
- **"₹20" / "₹25,000"**: Orange-gradient headline style with a rupee symbol.

## 3. Text Animation Rules
- **Headline IN (8–10 frames):** Scale 130 % → 95 % → 100 % (overshoot pop). Opacity 0 → 100. Directional motion blur, plus a slight 3D Y-rotation (−15° → 0°). It lands exactly on the spoken syllable, on the music beat.
- **Sub-word IN:** Starts 4–6 frames after the headline. Slides 20 px from the right with a fade, or a quick type-on.
- **Lower caption IN:** Word-by-word. Each word fades up with a blur-to-sharp (Gaussian blur 20 → 0) and a scale 90 → 100 %. The duotone mask wipes left → right over 10–12 frames, so the word shimmers from plum to blue.
- **OUT:** Fast blur-out with an opacity drop (5–6 frames), or the text is wiped away by the next transition. Headlines often stay on screen while the lower captions change underneath.
- **Hold:** The headline holds for the whole phrase (1.5–4 s) with a subtle 2–3 % slow scale-up drift.

## 4. Transitions & VFX
1. **White-silhouette intro flash (0.0–0.2 s):** The presenter's cut-out mask fills solid white for 2–3 frames, then reveals the clip. The "Ludhiana" text slams in from blurred.
2. **White light-leak/flash transitions** (overexposure burst, 6–8 frames, with a warm yellow light leak). Used at about 3.9 s, 6.8 s, 25.5 s and 30.6 s. During the flash, the text glitch-dissolves into white horizontal streaks.
3. **Digital glitch/datamosh** at about 18.0 s. Horizontal block displacement, pixel-sorting slices and RGB split for about 10 frames between the "Attractive rental" and "Luxury Heritage" scenes.
4. **Hard cuts on the beat** for the B-roll and the scene changes at about 5.0, 6.0, 12.8 and 21.2 s.
5. **Falling money overlay** (7.0–9.0 s): Paper banknotes fall and flutter down across the frame in 3D, with motion blur and depth of field, over the "Investment starts → ₹32 Lakh" shot.
6. **Punch-in zooms:** Instant 115–125 % scale jumps on emphasis lines, at about 2.0 s and 12.5 s.
7. **Dip to black** at about 36.8 s, fading into the end card.
8. **Global grade:** A slight bloom/glow on the highlights, plus a subtle vignette.

## 6. Sound Design
- **Music bed:** An upbeat, modern **house/corporate-pop groove at about 128 BPM**. A four-on-the-floor kick lands every 0.47 s from 0.7 s through about 36.5 s. Bright plucks/claps, no vocals. It is ducked about −14 dB under the voice (sidechain/auto-duck) and is loud enough to feel energetic. Cut all edits and text pops on the kick.
- **Voice:** A clean, close lav-mic voiceover. Confident, friendly and fast-paced (Hinglish salesperson-influencer tone). Compressed, de-essed, peaking around −6 dBFS with integrated loudness around −14 LUFS.
- **SFX list (sync exactly):**
  - **0.0 s:** Short **swoosh + bass impact** on the intro white flash.
  - **Each top-headline pop:** A soft, airy **whoosh/swish** (6–10 frames).
  - **Each lower duotone word:** A subtle **pop/click** or light "tick".
  - **3.5–3.9 s:** A **reverse riser/sweep into a white-flash impact** (cinematic hit with reverb tail).
  - **6.6–8.0 s:** A **cash register "cha-ching" + paper money flutter** as the notes fall.
  - **12.5 s:** A quick **zoom whoosh** on the punch-in.
  - **18.0 s:** A **digital glitch/data stutter** (8–10 frames).
  - **25.5 s and 30.6 s:** **Whoosh + soft impact** on the white flashes.
  - **35.5 s:** A **shimmer/sparkle** as the logo badge appears.
  - **36.8–43.6 s:** The music switches to an **elegant outro swell**: warm pads, soft piano/strings and light chimes with a gentle shimmer while the tagline types on (subtle typewriter ticks are optional). Fade out over the last 0.8 s.
