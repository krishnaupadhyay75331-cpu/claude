## 1. MASTER PROMPT (paste into an AI video agent or give it to an editor)

> Create a 63-second vertical 9:16 Instagram Reel at 24 fps in a cinematic **"emotional problem → expert solution → happy ending → CTA"** format, with a Hinglish voiceover from a male relationship/astrology consultant.
>
> **Two visual worlds alternate:**
> 1. **The "story" world.** Photoreal, AI-generated cinematic stills of a sad young Indian woman (early 20s, messy bun, dark teal/bottle-green cotton kurta, black leggings, barefoot) in warm, golden-hour Indian homes. Each still gets a slow Ken Burns push-in or pan and one animated overlay effect.
> 2. **The "expert" world.** A male presenter (about 30, black rectangular glasses, short beard, styled quiff, dark navy pinstripe shirt with sleeves rolled, red kalava thread and a watch on his wrists) sits in a beige armchair. The set is a moody dark-navy panelled study with wooden bookshelves, brass sextant and armillary-sphere décor, and soft key light from camera left. Crushed blacks, with a heavy black gradient over the bottom 35% of the frame for captions.
>
> **Editing style.** Cut on every sentence. Switch between a wide seated shot and a tight 3/4 side-profile close-up of the presenter looking off-camera right. Every scene change uses a **white flash with teal-green bokeh light-leak transition** (about 0.3–0.5 s: bokeh circles bloom, the frame goes to pure white for 4–6 frames, then fades into the next shot through more bokeh). Put a soft whoosh SFX on each flash.
>
> **Captions.** Keyword-style kinetic typography, not full subtitles. Show **one big hero word** in a clean geometric sans (Poppins Medium / Glacial Indifference style), white, about 110 px tall on a 1080 canvas. Place it in the lower-middle third, centered, with a soft dark drop shadow. **Tiny lowercase helper words** (about 28 px, same font, light weight) sit tucked above-right and below-left of the hero word. Each word appears exactly when it is spoken: a blur-to-sharp fade-in plus a scale from 105% to 100% over 6 frames. The hero word is replaced, never stacked. Special emphasis cards use a **teal (#1FA58A) rounded pill** behind white text, revealed left-to-right like a typewriter.
>
> **Grade.** Teal-and-orange. Story scenes are warm amber with soft window light. Expert scenes are cool navy with warm skin.
>
> **Music.** A soft, emotional, slow (about 70–80 BPM) ambient piano plus pad bed. Keep it low (about −24 LUFS under the voice) and constant, with a slight lift at the happy-ending dance scene and a resolve on the CTA.

## 7. EDIT WORKFLOW (CapCut / Premiere / After Effects)
1. Film the presenter in two setups (wide seated plus a tight side profile) and record a clean VO.
2. Cut the A-roll to the VO and remove all pauses (fast, no breaths).
3. Insert the story stills (animated) at the timestamps above.
4. Add the bokeh/white-flash transition at every A-roll ↔ B-roll switch.
5. Add word-level captions. Manually pick one hero word per phrase and keep the rest tiny.
6. Mask the presenter for the "10 SAAL" text-behind-subject effect (CapCut: Cutout → place text on a layer under it).
7. Grade with teal-orange and a bottom gradient. Mix the music at −24 LUFS under the VO and export 1080×1920, 24 fps, about 16 Mbps.
