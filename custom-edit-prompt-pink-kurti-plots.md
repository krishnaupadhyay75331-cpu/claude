## 5. Text animation, overlays and VFX

**Text animation** (the same rules as the reference):
- **Headline IN (8–10 frames):** scale 130% → 95% → 100% (an overshoot pop), opacity 0 → 100, directional motion blur, and a slight 3D Y-rotation from −15° to 0°. It lands **exactly on the spoken syllable**, on the music beat.
- **Sub-word IN:** starts 4–6 frames after the headline. It slides 20 px in from the right with a fade, or types on quickly.
- **Lower caption IN:** word by word. Each word fades up from blurred to sharp (Gaussian blur 20 → 0) and scales 90 → 100%. The duotone mask wipes left to right over 10–12 frames, so the word shimmers from pink to blue.
- **Hold:** the headline holds for the whole phrase (1.5–4 s) with a 2–3% slow scale-up drift. Headlines often **stay on screen while the lower captions change underneath**.
- **OUT:** a fast blur-out with an opacity drop (5–6 frames), or the text is wiped away by the next transition.

**Overlays** (PNG sequences or MOVs with alpha):
